Document Requirements

The documents required vary by loan type and lender. Common documents include:

  • KYC Documents (PAN Card, Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport)
  • Address Proof (Utility bills, Rental agreement)
  • Income Proof (Salary slips, ITR, Bank statements)
  • Employment or Business Proof
  • Property documents (for Home Loan & LAP)

Many lending partners accept online document submission through their secure portals. You can upload scanned copies or clear photos of your documents. We recommend keeping original documents ready for verification when required.

Yes, PAN card is generally mandatory for all loan applications in India as it is required for financial transactions and KYC compliance. If you don't have a PAN card, we recommend applying for one before starting the loan process.

Repayment Questions

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is calculated based on the loan amount, interest rate, and tenure. You can use our EMI Calculator to get an estimate of your monthly payments.

Yes, most lenders allow prepayment or foreclosure of loans, though there may be charges involved. The terms and conditions for prepayment vary by lender and loan type. We recommend checking the prepayment terms before signing the loan agreement.

Missing an EMI payment can result in late payment fees, penalty interest, and a negative impact on your credit score. It's important to communicate with your lender if you anticipate difficulty in making payments, as they may offer alternative arrangements.
