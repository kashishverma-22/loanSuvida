ELIGIBILITY & DOCUMENTS

Know Before You Apply

Eligibility and documentation requirements can vary depending on the lending partner and loan product.

Basic Eligibility

  • Business should meet the lender's eligibility criteria.
  • Applicant should satisfy applicable age and profile requirements.
  • Business vintage may be considered by the lending partner.
  • Business turnover and financial profile may be evaluated.
  • Credit history may be considered during the application process.
  • Final eligibility is subject to lender assessment.

Documents Required

  • PAN Card and identity proof.
  • Address proof of applicant/business.
  • Business registration or relevant business proof.
  • Bank account statements.
  • Income tax returns or financial documents, wherever applicable.
  • Additional documents may be requested by the lender.
