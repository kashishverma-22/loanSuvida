FACTORS THAT AFFECT RATES

What Determines Your Loan Interest Rate?

Understand the key factors that influence the interest rate offered to you.

CIBIL Score

Higher credit scores (750+) typically qualify for lower interest rates. Lower scores may result in higher rates.

Income & Repayment Capacity

Higher income and stable employment often lead to better rates as they indicate lower risk to lenders.

Loan Tenure

Shorter tenures may attract lower rates, while longer tenures could have higher rates due to increased risk.

Loan Amount

Higher loan amounts may qualify for better rates, depending on the lender's policies and your profile.

Employment Type

Salaried individuals with stable jobs may get better rates compared to self-employed applicants.

Lender Policies

Each lender has its own pricing policies, risk assessment models, and rate benchmarks.

SMART BORROWING

How to Get the Best Interest Rate?

  • Maintain a CIBIL score above 750
  • Compare rates from multiple lenders
  • Opt for a shorter tenure if possible
  • Have a stable income and employment
  • Keep your debt-to-income ratio low
