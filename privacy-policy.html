1. Introduction

Welcome to Loan Suvidha. We value the trust you place in us when you use our website, submit an enquiry, request information or explore loan-related services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared and protected when you interact with Loan Suvidha through our website and related services.

By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the service or interaction, we may collect different categories of information.

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number and other information voluntarily provided by you.
  • Loan Information: Preferred loan type, requested loan amount, employment information and other details relevant to your enquiry.
  • Financial Information: Where necessary for a specific loan enquiry, financial or income-related information may be requested.
  • Identification Information: Certain documents or identification details may be required by lending partners during the application process.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system and website interaction data.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, approximate session activity, links clicked and other website interactions.

3. Information You Provide

Information may be provided when you voluntarily interact with our website, including when you:

  • Submit a loan enquiry or application form.
  • Contact our team by phone or email.
  • Request information about a loan product.
  • Use calculators, enquiry forms or other website features.
  • Subscribe to communications or updates.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our website may be used for purposes such as:

  • Processing and responding to your loan enquiry.
  • Understanding your requirements and preferences.
  • Connecting you with suitable lending partners, where applicable.
  • Communicating with you about your enquiry, application or requested services.
  • Improving our website, products and user experience.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

5. Loan Partners & Financial Institutions

Loan Suvidha may assist users in exploring suitable loan options through lending partners, including banks, NBFCs and other financial institutions.

Where you request assistance with a loan product, relevant information may be shared with an appropriate lending partner for the purpose of evaluating or processing your enquiry.

Loan approval, interest rates, eligibility, documentation and final terms are determined by the respective lending institution and may vary based on its policies.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not intend to sell or rent your personal information as a commercial product. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary for providing requested services or fulfilling legal obligations.

  • Lending Partners: Banks, NBFCs and financial institutions involved in processing your enquiry.
  • Service Providers: Vendors supporting website hosting, technology, communication, analytics or related services.
  • Professional Advisors: Where necessary for legitimate business, compliance or legal purposes.
  • Legal Authorities: Where disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful request.

We aim to limit information sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Secure Transmission

Appropriate security measures may be used while information is transmitted.

Protected Systems

Information is handled using reasonable technical safeguards.

Access Control

Access may be restricted to authorized persons where appropriate.

Security Updates

Systems and processes may be reviewed and updated when necessary.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted through the internet.

8. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, provide requested services, maintain business records, resolve disputes or comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

When information is no longer reasonably required, appropriate steps may be taken to delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable requirements.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and circumstances, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

  • Access: Request information about personal data we may hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion where legally permissible.
  • Communication Preferences: Request to stop certain promotional communications.
  • Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may request withdrawal of consent, subject to applicable limitations.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us at info@loansuvida.com.

10. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage and improve your browsing experience.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for certain website functions.
  • Analytics Cookies: May help us understand how visitors use our website.
  • Preference Cookies: May remember certain user preferences.
  • Marketing Technologies: Where used, these may help provide relevant communication or advertising.

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

11. Communication Preferences

If you provide your contact details while submitting an enquiry, we may use them to respond to your request or provide information related to the service you requested.

Where promotional communication is sent, you may request to opt out of such communication by contacting us or following the available unsubscribe instructions.

12. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including websites operated by lending partners, service providers or other organizations.

Once you leave our website and access a third-party website, that website's own privacy policy and practices will apply. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of every external website you visit.

13. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information through our website, they may contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be considered.

14. Fraud & Security Awareness

Protecting your information also requires your cooperation. Please avoid sharing sensitive passwords, OTPs, PINs or banking credentials with unknown individuals claiming to represent Loan Suvidha.

Important Security Reminder

Never share your OTP, password, ATM PIN, UPI PIN or other confidential banking credentials with anyone.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, business practices or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page along with a revised "Last Updated" date where appropriate.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to remain informed about how your information is handled.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, requests or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact us.
