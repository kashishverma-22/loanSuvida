Personal Loan Documents

Documents required for a personal loan application.

Identity Proof

PAN Card, Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License

Any one

Address Proof

Aadhaar, Utility Bill, Rental Agreement, Passport

Any one

Income Proof

Salary slips (last 3 months), Bank statements, ITR

Salaried / Self-employed

Bank Statements

Last 6 months bank statements showing income and transactions

Required

Passport Size Photograph

Recent colored photograph (2 copies)

Required

Self-employed applicants may need to provide additional income proof such as ITR, business registration, and bank statements.
