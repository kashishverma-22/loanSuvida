PRO TIPS

How to Improve Your Eligibility

Simple steps to increase your chances of loan approval.

01

Maintain a Good CIBIL Score

Pay your credit card bills and EMIs on time. A score above 750 improves your chances significantly.

02

Reduce Existing Debt

Lower your debt-to-income ratio by clearing existing loans before applying for a new one.

03

Stable Employment & Income

Lenders prefer applicants with steady income and at least 1-2 years of job stability.

04

Accurate Documentation

Ensure all your KYC, income, and address documents are updated and complete.
