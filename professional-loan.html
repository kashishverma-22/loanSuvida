ELIGIBILITY & DOCUMENTS

Check Your Eligibility

Basic requirements may vary depending on the lender and applicant profile.

Basic Eligibility

  • Applicant should meet the lender's minimum age criteria.
  • Applicant should have a suitable professional profile.
  • Stable income or professional earnings may be required.
  • Credit history may be considered.
  • Final eligibility is decided by the lending partner.

Documents Generally Required

  • Identity and address proof
  • PAN and Aadhaar or other accepted KYC
  • Bank account statements
  • Income proof / financial documents
  • Professional qualification or registration documents, where applicable

Professional Loan FAQs

Find answers to commonly asked questions.

A professional loan is a financing solution that may be available to eligible professionals for business, practice-related or other permitted financial requirements, subject to lender terms.

Eligible professionals such as Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, doctors and other qualifying professionals may explore professional loan options. Eligibility depends on the lending partner.

Documents may include identity and address proof, PAN, bank statements, income documents and professional registration or qualification documents. Exact requirements vary by lender.

Eligible doctors may explore professional loan options depending on their profile, income, credit history and the criteria of the lending partner.

The interest rate may depend on factors such as applicant profile, credit history, income, loan amount, tenure and lender policies.

Processing time depends on the lender, document verification, eligibility and other application requirements. There is no guaranteed approval timeline.
